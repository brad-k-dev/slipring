Privacy Policy

Slipring  ·  last updated 29 September 2026

Slipring does not collect any data.

There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no account system and no third-party SDK of any kind in this app. It makes no network requests. It works with the device in airplane mode, because it never had anything to send.

What is stored

Your best score, and a flag recording that you have tapped once, are written to the app's own storage on your device. They never leave the device, cannot be read by any other app, and are deleted when you delete the app.

Sharing

The share button on the game over screen hands your score to iOS's standard share sheet. What happens from there is between you and whichever app you pick. Slipring itself sends nothing.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and treats every player identically. It collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page changes with it and the date above is updated. A version that collected anything would be a different app from this one.
